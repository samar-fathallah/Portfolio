Projects

Master's Thesis — Adversarial Data Augmentation for Autonomous Driving

Master's thesis addressing the failure of end-to-end driving models in safety-critical scenarios — cases where real-world training data cannot be collected. Built a diffusion-based augmentation pipeline in PyTorch that synthesized collision and near-collision scenarios from the nuScenes dataset by injecting adversarial vehicles, then fine-tuned planning-oriented driving models on the augmented data. Reduced collision rate by 39% across both open-loop and closed-loop evaluation frameworks. Co-authored paper under review at a top-tier international conference on robot learning.

Semantic-Guided GAN for Adverse-Weather Synthesis

Master's research project extending a DP-GAN framework for semantic image synthesis under adverse weather conditions. Introduced a novel fusion strategy, dual SPADE normalization with a learnable fusion mechanism embedded within a three-pyramid generator architecture that integrates clear-weather features with semantic segmentation maps. Validated the synthesized images as augmentation for a downstream segmentation model, achieving the best FID score of 98.01 among all tested baselines.

Automatic Music Transcription — Bachelor Thesis

End-to-end system converting audio recordings of musical notes into readable sheet music, combining signal processing and preprocessing with deep learning. Designed the audio preprocessing stage using Fourier transforms, feeding representations into deep learning models for note recognition. Compared DNN, CNN, and LSTM architectures; the CNN achieved the best performance at 75% average precision. Deployed as a web platform where users upload audio and download the corresponding sheet music.
